# Compatibility

Games RecompStation can play through a launcher: a recompilation or PC port it installs for you. Each game names its launchers, linked to the projects of the people who made them. You always need your own copy of each game; RecompStation never supplies game files.

The launcher links come from the catalog: after adding launchers, run `python3 tools/catalog/compatibility_credits.py`.
